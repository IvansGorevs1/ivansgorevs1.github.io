1. Who is responsible for your data

Mise is developed and operated by Ivans Gorevs, an individual developer based in Latvia (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). The Developer is the data controller for personal data processed through Mise, except where a third-party provider acts as an independent controller for its own processing.

Privacy contact: miseappcontact@gmail.com

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mise mobile application and this Privacy Policy webpage process information. It applies when you scan or manually enter ingredients, generate or view recipes, load recipe images, save recipes, use cooking features, report generated content, receive advertisements, change privacy choices, or contact us.

Mise currently does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization. If those features are introduced, this policy will be updated before they are made available.

3. Information Mise processes

Photos you choose to submit

If you take or select a photo for ingredient recognition, Mise uploads the photo to our server and sends it to the OpenAI API for analysis. A photo may contain personal data depending on what is visible. Avoid submitting images that show people, documents, addresses, screens, or other unnecessary personal details.

Ingredient and recipe request data

Mise processes detected or manually entered ingredient names, quantities and units, selected recipe preferences, requested recipe count, and app language to provide the requested feature. Recipe-generation data is sent to DeepSeek. The original photo is not sent to DeepSeek.

Content reports

When you report an AI-generated recipe, Mise sends and stores the selected report category, any optional comment, the reported recipe and its content, related ingredient and preference context, language, app version, platform, source screen, and a request identifier. A report does not require an account, name, or email address. Reports are stored in Mise’s PostgreSQL database hosted through Railway.

Technical, security, and diagnostic data

Our server and hosting provider may process an IP address, request time, endpoint, request identifier, response status, app version, platform, provider status, and limited error or diagnostic information. We use this information to deliver requests, apply rate limits, prevent abuse, keep Mise secure, and troubleshoot failures.

Privacy-focused usage analytics

Mise sends Aptabase a limited set of events, such as app start, onboarding completion, feature use, success or failure status, item or recipe counts, and whether a recipe was opened from results, history, or favorites. We do not deliberately send photos, ingredient names, recipe names, recipe text, selected recipe preferences, comments, email addresses, or raw error messages to Aptabase.

Aptabase does not provide us with a persistent device identifier. Its server creates a daily pseudonymous identifier from the IP address, user agent, an app-specific salt, and a salt that is rotated every 24 hours. Aptabase may also process technical information such as app version, operating system, country inferred from the network connection, and event time.

Error monitoring

Server errors and limited diagnostic context may be sent to Sentry. Mise is configured not to deliberately include uploaded photos, ingredient lists, recipe content, content-report text, authentication secrets, or user profiles in Sentry events. Despite these controls, diagnostic providers may receive ordinary technical data such as an IP address, request metadata, and device or network information.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, user interactions, diagnostic information, device and app information, advertising ID, app set ID or other device/account identifiers, and your advertising privacy choices. Processing depends on your location, device settings, consent choices, Google’s configuration, and whether personalized, non-personalized, limited, or test ads are served.

Recipe image search and delivery

To find an illustrative recipe image, Mise may send recipe-related search keywords—such as a recipe title, dish description, or ingredient terms—to the Pexels and Unsplash APIs. Mise does not send your original food photo to these providers. When your device loads an image from a provider or its content delivery network, that provider may receive your IP address, user agent, device or browser information, requested image URL, and request time.

Local app data

Favorites, recipe history, app settings, language, onboarding status, and similar preferences are stored on your device. Advertising consent choices may also be stored locally by Google’s SDK. This local data is not synchronized to a Mise account because Mise does not currently offer accounts.

Support and privacy communications

If you email us, we process your email address, message, and any information you choose to include so that we can respond and maintain an appropriate record of the request.

Privacy Policy webpage data

This page is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Mise does not place its own analytics code or advertising on this page. GitHub may nevertheless process ordinary server-log and technical data when the page is visited, such as an IP address, user agent, requested URL, and time of access.

4. How we use information

We process information to:

  • recognize ingredients and generate the recipes you request;
  • display relevant illustrative recipe images and proper attribution;
  • save selected content and preferences locally on your device;
  • review reports about unsafe, inaccurate, or inappropriate AI-generated content and improve safeguards;
  • operate, secure, monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot Mise;
  • understand aggregate feature usage and improve the app;
  • display and measure advertising, manage consent, and prevent advertising fraud;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
  • comply with applicable law and protect users, the Developer, and third parties.

Providing a photo is optional because ingredients can be entered manually. Ingredient and preference data is necessary only when you ask Mise to generate a recipe. If you do not provide the data needed for a selected feature, Mise may be unable to provide that feature.

7. Data retention

  • Temporary photos on Mise’s server: deleted after the image-recognition request finishes, including when processing fails.
  • OpenAI API data: OpenAI states that API abuse-monitoring logs are retained for up to 30 days by default, unless longer retention is required by law or is reasonably necessary to protect its services or third parties. The exact treatment may depend on the API endpoint and the data controls applied to the Developer’s account.
  • Recipe requests: Mise does not ordinarily retain ingredient and preference data on its server after the request is completed, except where the recipe and related context are included in a content report, or limited information must be retained for security, diagnostics, or legal reasons. DeepSeek determines its own provider-side retention under the Open Platform terms and applicable arrangements.
  • Content reports: retained while reasonably necessary to review and document the reported issue, improve safety and reliability, identify repeated misuse, resolve disputes, or comply with law. The retention decision considers the report’s status, seriousness, sensitivity, risk of recurrence, and legal relevance. Reports are deleted or anonymized when they are no longer reasonably needed.
  • Aptabase analytics: Aptabase states that analytics records may be stored for up to five years. Because daily identifiers rotate and no persistent Mise account is attached, we generally cannot identify which Aptabase records relate to a particular person.
  • Sentry events and server logs: retained for the period configured for the relevant service or plan and only as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, service operation, and legal compliance.
  • Local app data: favorites, recipe history, and settings remain on your device until you remove them in Mise, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Support and privacy emails: retained as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain a record of the request, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Camera and photo-library permissions

Mise requests access to the camera or photo library only when you choose the corresponding feature. You can deny or revoke these permissions in your device settings and continue using manual ingredient entry. Mise does not require access to contacts, the microphone, or precise device location for its recipe features.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal data; restrict or object to processing; request portability; and withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

  • You can avoid photo processing by entering ingredients manually.
  • You can remove favorites and history in Mise, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • You can use Privacy choices in Mise when the option is available to review or change advertising consent choices. You can also manage or reset advertising identifiers through Android settings.
  • You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will stop that processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds or the processing is needed for legal claims.
  • You can contact us at miseappcontact@gmail.com to exercise a privacy right or request deletion of a content report.

Because Mise has no accounts and some data is local, aggregated, or identified only by short-lived technical identifiers, we may be unable to associate some records with you. We may request enough information to verify, locate, and securely handle a request, but we will not ask for more information than reasonably necessary.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia or another competent data protection authority.

11. Children’s privacy

Mise is not directed to children under 13 or to children below the minimum age at which they may independently consent to data processing in their country. A child below the applicable age should use Mise only with authorization from a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly request a child’s name, contact details, or account information, and Mise does not currently offer accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal data without the required authorization, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12. Automated processing and AI output

Mise uses artificial intelligence to identify ingredients and generate recipes. This processing is performed to provide the feature you request. Mise does not use personal data to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

AI-generated results can be incomplete or inaccurate. Food-safety warnings and any terms governing reliance on recipes are presented separately in Mise and, where applicable, in the Terms of Use. You can report unsafe, inaccurate, or inappropriate generated content from within the app.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including encrypted network connections, restricted server credentials, request-size limits, rate limits, provider timeouts, and minimization of information sent to analytics and monitoring services. Access to content reports is limited to purposes related to review, safety, service operation, and legal compliance. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Sale of data and advertising-related sharing

We do not sell your food photos, ingredient lists, recipes, content reports, or contact information for money. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive the advertising and technical data described above to provide, personalize, measure, and secure ads. Some privacy laws may define certain personalized-advertising disclosures as a “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising.” Where applicable, you can use the privacy options provided in Mise or contact us to exercise an available opt-out right.
